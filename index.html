Company Overview

Gold Line Shipping LTD is a Haifa, Israel–based maritime logistics provider founded in 1996 by Mr. Jacob Ben-Shushan. The company specializes in port agency services, military and navy support, project cargo handling, and RORO / vehicle transportation.

With over 28 years of experience, the company provides 24/7 crew changes and technical assistance to global and local clients.
